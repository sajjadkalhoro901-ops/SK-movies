# 🎬 SK Movies

**SK Movies — Your Movie & Anime World**

A clean, mobile-friendly entertainment hub for discovering movies and anime, with search, categories, ratings, trailers, sharing and legal viewing options.

## ✨ Features

- 🎥 Hollywood, Bollywood, South Indian & Classic movies
- 🍥 Anime and Donghua collection
- 🌐 Japanese, Chinese, English and Hindi language information
- 🔎 Search and category filters
- ⭐ Ratings and genres
- ▶️ Trailer links
- 📺 Legal viewing search
- 📤 Social sharing
- 📱 Mobile-friendly design
- 📞 WhatsApp and Facebook contact options

## 🛡️ Safety

SK Movies does not host or distribute movie files. Viewing buttons point toward legal availability/search services, while trailer buttons point to YouTube search results.

## 🌟 Brand

**SK Movies** is the site's entertainment brand: simple, clean and easy to remember.
